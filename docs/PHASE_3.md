# Phase 3 — All Four Cars and Surface Physics

## What was built

| Area | Where | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Four cars | `src/physics/VehicleParams.ts` (Volt), `src/physics/cars/{gravel,frost,dune}.ts`, registry `src/data/cars.ts` | One shared physics core, four parameter sets. Each car also has a camera style, particle style, engine voice and default livery. Each track declares its car (`car` in the level JSON); `?car=<id>` overrides (dev). |
| Behaviour modules | `src/physics/CarBehavior.ts` | Hooks `update / onLeaveGround / onLand` for the bits a number cannot express: **Gravel** closed-loop power-slide assist, **Frost** ice/snow momentum + studs, **Dune** spring-launch off lips and bouncy touch-down. Deterministic, state-only. |
| Per-surface tyre model | `src/physics/Surface.ts`, `Vehicle.applyTyres` | Every surface now has `peakSlip` (slip angle of peak grip: loose ground peaks late), `slideKeep` (grip kept in a slide: ice falls off a cliff, dirt barely) and `loose`. Cars add `surfaceGrip[surface]` and `surfaceRolling[surface]` multipliers. |
| Procedural models | `src/render/cars/*`, `CarView.ts` | Distinct silhouettes: **Volt** low stadium wedge, **Gravel** tall rally hatch (flares, roof scoop, light pod, big wing, knobbly tyres), **Frost** 4x4 pickup (roof rack, light bar, bull bar, studded tyres), **Dune** open tube-frame buggy (rear engine, whip flag, balloon tyres). Wheels follow the suspension per car. |
| Livery | `src/core/Livery.ts`, menu "Car" section | 10 paint presets + custom colour, 6 accent presets + custom; saved per car, applied live. |
| Camera per car | `CameraStyle` in `src/data/cars.ts`, `CameraRig.style` | Gravel close and shaky, Frost heavy and lazy, Dune high with a big FOV kick, Volt tight. |
| Particles | `src/render/Particles.ts`, `WheelFx.ts` | Pooled soft-point sprites (one draw call): dust on dirt/grass, big plumes on sand, snow spray, tyre smoke on hard ground when sliding, touch-down bursts. Rate scales with speed and slip; per-car amount/size. |
| Audio per car | `AudioEngine` + `EngineVoice` | Four engine voices (waveforms, ratio, filter sweep, gearbox, vibrato). Road noise by surface (crunch on loose ground, hiss on snow, hum on tarmac), tyre squeal only on hard ground. |
| Test tracks | `src/data/levels/{gravel01,frost01,dune01}.json` | **Dirt Road** (Gravel: dirt, grass, sand, chicanes, banked turn, kicker jump), **Ice Pass** (Frost: snow and ice with rails, slopes, banked turn, jump), **Dune Run** (Dune: sand, dips, big jump, banked turn, kicker). Generated by `scripts/make-levels.ts` and bot-validated. |
| Tests | `tests/cars.test.ts` | 13 new (68 total): roster, per-surface model, and blind-test measurables below. |
| Tuning labs | `scripts/car-lab.ts`, `scripts/drift-lab.ts` | Acceleration, braking, cornering and jump comparisons per car and surface; drift/step-steer behaviour on home surfaces. |

## How to test

```bash
npm run dev      # http://localhost:5173 ; pick a level in the menu (Esc) or use ?level=gravel01 | frost01 | dune01 | sprint01
npm test         # 68 tests
npx tsx scripts/car-lab.ts     # compare the four cars
npx tsx scripts/drift-lab.ts   # drift character on home surfaces
```

Try, in this order, on the same flat stretch of each level: full-throttle launch, then a brake-tap or flick into a corner:
- **Volt** (Sprint 01): grips and turns in instantly, long drifts on a quick counter-steer.
- **Gravel** (Dirt Road): loose and lively; throttle plus lock starts a ~35 degree power slide that settles instead of spinning.
- **Frost** (Ice Pass): heavy and slow to turn; low grip but the studs bite (it stops and pulls away on ice better than any other car); long wide drifts.
- **Dune** (Dune Run): light and fast on sand, wide arcs, and noticeably longer and higher jumps.
Open the menu to change paint; the car recolours live.

## Tuning results (`scripts/car-lab.ts`)

Peak centripetal acceleration (g' = 19.6 m/s^2) at 30 m/s with a gentle steering ramp, using the velocity-vector turn rate so spins cannot inflate it:

| | tarmac | dirt | snow | ice (20 m/s) | sand |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Volt | **2.30** | 1.19 | 0.80 | 0.28 | 0.94 |
| Gravel | 1.38 | **1.67** | 0.84 | 0.21 | 1.05 |
| Frost | 1.25 | 1.07 | **1.01** | **0.39** | 0.82 |
| Dune | 1.43 | 1.46 | 0.85 | 0.21 | **1.61** |

0-100 km/h on tarmac: Volt 2.7 s, Dune 2.6 s, Gravel 2.9 s, Frost 3.2 s (on sand Dune 2.6 s vs Volt 3.1 s; on ice Frost 3.5 s vs 5.7 - 7.5 s for the rest). Braking 25 to 0 m/s on ice: Frost 46 m, Volt 58 m, Gravel 74 m, Dune 76 m. Same ramp, full throttle: air time Volt 2.0 s, Gravel 2.2 s, Frost 2.2 s, Dune 2.5 s (apex 13 m vs 8 m). These relationships are asserted in `tests/cars.test.ts`, so a later tweak that blurs the cars will fail a test.

## Design decisions

- **Behaviours are closed-loop where they push yaw.** My first Gravel power-slide was a raw torque; it spun the car in chicanes. It now drives the yaw rate toward ~1.35x the kinematic rate for the current steering angle and fades as body slip grows, so it starts a slide without spinning.
- **Volt is deliberately poor on loose ground** (`surfaceGrip` 0.7-0.8) so each specialist has a surface it owns; otherwise Volt's huge base grip beat every car everywhere.
- **Dune's "floaty" is three things**: low mass and big wheels, `airGravityScale` 0.86, and a speed-proportional spring-launch kick when it leaves the ground (capped), plus a small rebound on hard landings.
- **Slippery tracks get rails.** Frost's course has walls on both sides of every snow and ice piece; with a heavy car on 0.4 grip ice, drivers (and the validation bot) need something to bounce off. Dirt and sand courses do not.
- **Cornering metric**: yaw rate x speed overstates grip for a car that spins while sliding straight, so all lateral-grip comparisons (lab and tests) use the turn rate of the velocity vector.
- **Bot hints in the level JSON** (`bot: { maxSpeed, lookTime, steerSlew, lateralBudget }`): the validation bot now takes the car's real grip (friction per surface, braking from the slipperiest surface ahead), and each level records the driving style that finishes it, so tools and tests reproduce the same run.

## Known issues / not yet done

- Feel was tuned with numbers, a bot, and headless metrics, not hands on a real keyboard/pad. The values most likely to want a human pass: Gravel's power-slide strength, Frost's steering rate, Dune's launch kick and air-control authority. All are live-tunable in the `?debug=1` panel (per-car, saved separately) and the surface grip multipliers have sliders too.
- Test tracks are short (bot times 32-46 s). Frost's bot time is the longest because the bot is cautious on ice; a human should be quicker.
- Particles are CPU-updated points (1400 pool): cheap, but no sorting/soft depth fade yet; skid marks, sparks and wheel-dust trails are Phase 5.
- Engine voices are four hand-picked oscillator settings; the full audio pass (turbo whistle, surface-specific tyre layers, music) is Phase 8.
- Hood cam shows the Volt hood well; the Gravel/Frost/Dune hood views are functional but not individually polished.
- Per-car **special-block** behaviour is identical (boosts, etc. apply the same acceleration to all cars).
