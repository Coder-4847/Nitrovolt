## How to test

```bash
npm run dev                      # default level: Sprint 01
# ?level=circuit01 | boostlab | stunt01      (also ?track=greybox / flat for the Phase 1 test courses)
# ?debug=1&autodrive=1             bot drives (great for watching the pads and the finish panel)
npm test
npx tsx scripts/make-levels.ts   # re-chain the levels, re-validate with the bot, rewrite medal times
```

What to look at:
1. **Sprint 01**: 3-2-1 countdown (menu: Instant / Quick 1.2 s / Classic 3 s), splits with deltas on the second run, finish panel, medal. `Enter` restarts instantly, `Backspace` respawns at the last checkpoint (clock keeps running).
2. **Boost Lab**: drive over each pad in turn. Turbo/Turbo 2 (orange/red chevrons) push past the normal top speed; Reactor (purple) pushes *and* lifts; No-Steer / No-Brake / Engine Off lock that control; Cruise holds your entry speed; Slow-Mo slows physics time; Gravity (pink) lowers gravity for 4 s; the three Fragile (cream, cracked) tiles at the end shatter if you land on them from a height and are back after a restart.
3. **Ring Road**: 2 laps, lap line splits.
4. **Stunt Park**: loop, half-pipe, spiral descent, wall-ride, kicker + landing. The path bot finishes it in about 26 s.

## Design decisions

- **Grid**: cell = 16 m, level = 4 m. A block enters through the south edge of its anchor cell (rot 0 = north). Curve radii **8 / 24 / 40** and the chicane angles (36.87 deg / 53.13 deg on R=40) are chosen so every exit lands exactly on a cell edge; `tests/blocks.test.ts` proves it for every piece in every variant.
- **Slopes and ramps are solved, not hand-tuned**: `solveSlope(forward, rise, R, arcs)` finds the angle so a block covers whole cells and whole levels. Jump ramps leave pitched up (about 25-30 deg), landings enter pitched down and level out; `gap` / `dy` in the chain place the landing.
- **Effects live on the road surface**, not on block types, so any block can be a boost pad. Colliders are grouped per (surface, effect); each fragile block is its own collider so it can break alone.
- **Effect numbers** (all in `Effects.ts`): Turbo +17 m/s^2 for 2.6 s (cap 1.3x top speed), Turbo 2 +28 m/s^2 for 3.4 s (cap 1.75x), Reactor +24 m/s^2 and 62 % anti-gravity lift for 2.4 s, No-Steer/No-Brake 0.25 s linger, Engine Off 0.5 s, Cruise captures speed on entry (10-70 m/s), Slow-Mo scales the physics step to 0.35 (the run clock keeps real time), Gravity 0.42x for 4 s. Fragile breaks when landing at more than 7 m/s.
- **Slow-motion scales the physics step** (and Rapier's timestep), not the tick count, so it stays deterministic and replay-safe.
- **Timing**: the clock starts at GO and counts 100 Hz ticks, but every checkpoint / finish time is **interpolated inside the tick** at the moment the car crosses the plane, rounded to whole milliseconds. The HUD clock is interpolated too so it matches the drawn car.
- **Countdown**: restart still resets in a single frame (instant). The default 3-2-1 is the 1.2 s "Quick" option; "Instant" starts running immediately, "Classic" is 3 s. The car is held (driver input ignored) until GO; respawn is disabled before GO.
- **Anti-cheat (basic)**: checkpoints must be collected in order, only forward crossings count, a finish line is ignored until all checkpoints of the lap are done, and on lap tracks the line only re-arms after the car is 40 m away from it. Respawns are counted and shown, not forbidden (time keeps running).
- **Respawn pose** is the last checkpoint line (or the start), zero speed, facing the line direction.
- **Mesh fix**: twisted road quads (banked entries) used to grow a ridge up to 0.45 m along the triangle diagonal, which launched cars at banked seams. Twisted quads are now split around their centre vertex and banked sweeps use finer frames.
- **Ground-align torque** (`groundAlign`): while only 1-3 wheels touch, the chassis is pulled toward the average contact normal so it sticks to banks, loops and corner landings.
- **Wall-ride is a 45 deg banked 64 m piece with 2.6 m rails**, not a vertical wall. Near-vertical walls needed far more work (the road rolled out from under the car at speed); this version holds at every speed from 30 to 66 m/s and is covered by a test. Steeper wall-rides are a Phase 6 set-piece task.
- **Path bot** (`PathBot`) is a dev tool that grew into a real driver: braking profile from curvature/surface lookahead, steering relative to the *velocity* vector (robust in slides), analog steering slew, lifts when sliding, backs out when stuck, loops closed circuits. It is the seed of the Phase 6 validator.

## Known issues / not yet done

- Level design is bot-validated but hand-balanced: a turbo pad right before a tight corner cannot be shed (boost beats brakes), so pads belong before straights. The Phase 6 generator must respect that, and size jump gaps from entry speed.
- Medal times in the JSON are provisional (bot time x 1.0 / 1.08 / 1.2 / 1.45). Real medal derivation and the medal-earned animation are Phase 4/6.
- Block thumbnails and level thumbnails are not generated yet (`thumbnail` is in the schema). There is no in-game level browser yet, only the menu dropdown (Phase 4).
- Hood-cam, ghosts and replays are untouched; the countdown length will be stored with ghosts in Phase 4.
- Loops need roughly 48 m/s to complete at R=24 (put at least 2-3 straight cells before one). Wall-ride entry at very low speed (< 25 m/s) slides down.
- Special blocks only exist for the Volt so far; per-car surface tuning is Phase 3.
- Gamepad / tilt / haptics remain hardware-untested (as in Phase 1).
